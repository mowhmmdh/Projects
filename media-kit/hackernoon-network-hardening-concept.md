# HackerNoon Submission Concept — Mohammad Hossein Asgari Somarin

## Title
Why Network Hardening Is More Than a Firewall

## Story angle
A practical, vendor-neutral explanation of how everyday infrastructure decisions determine a network's attack surface and incident blast radius.

## Structure
1. Why firewalls are only one control layer
2. Build an asset and service baseline
3. Apply least privilege to infrastructure administration
4. Remove or restrict unnecessary exposure
5. Segment users, servers, management, guest and IoT/CCTV networks
6. Treat patching and recoverable backups as security controls
7. Centralize useful logs and validate changes
8. A compact hardening checklist
