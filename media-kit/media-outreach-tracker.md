## Editorial constraints

- Do not claim publication before an article is actually live.
- Do not reuse the same article simultaneously where a publication requires exclusivity or first-publication rights.
- Do not include confidential infrastructure details, internal IP addresses, credentials, private company configurations, or customer information.
- Pitches should be tailored to each outlet's stated audience and submission rules.
- Any article submitted to an outlet must be original and should comply with that outlet's AI/content policy.
